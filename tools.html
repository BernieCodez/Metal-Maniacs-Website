Getting Started
How Teams Work

Everything in the suite is organized around teams. Create one, invite your crew, and all your data stays shared and in sync.

01
Create a Free Account

Sign up in seconds — no subscription, no credit card. Your account is what ties you to your teams and saves your work across sessions.

02
Create or Join a Team

Start your own team and invite others with a shareable join code, or paste a code to jump into an existing team instantly.

03
Manage Your Team

As a team owner you can view all members, remove people, set a default team for quick access, and switch between multiple teams on the dashboard.

Multiple Teams Belong to more than one team at once — useful for captains mentoring junior squads or members competing on two rosters.
Join with a Code No email invites needed. The team owner shares a short code and anyone with an account can join immediately.
Data Stays with the Team Notebook entries, schedules, and events are automatically scoped to the team — every member sees the same live data.
